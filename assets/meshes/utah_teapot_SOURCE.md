# Utah Teapot Mesh Source

This OBJ is generated from the official University of Utah Graphics Lab Utah
Teapot generator:

https://graphics.cs.utah.edu/teapot/

Generation settings:

- Version: 2026 - Cem Yuksel
- Model: triangle mesh
- Orientation: z-up
- Resolution: 8
- Enabled parts: body, lid, spout, handle
- Shape settings: Blinn scale, circular, trimmed, chamfer, curvature continuous
- Bottom: round
- Interior: Cem Yuksel
- Vertices: welded

The source page states that the Utah Teapot model files are freely available for
any use, including commercial use. It requests that derived versions identify the
model as the Utah Teapot and acknowledge its origin as the historic Utah Teapot
developed at the University of Utah.
